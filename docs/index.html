Invoicing

Professional invoices, posted the moment you issue them.

Create an invoice once. Zycount numbers it, works out SST, posts the double entry and keeps the receipt, the e-Invoice status and the ledger linked to it.

  • Gap-free numbering — per company, per year, editable to match your existing series.
  • See the journal before it posts — account by account, proved to balance.
  • Track every payment — receipts knock off invoices and feed customer statements and aging.
  • Print or download PDF — and submit to LHDN MyInvois from the same screen.

Questions

Asked by accountants, answered plainly.

Is Zycount real double-entry accounting?

Yes. Every transaction is a journal with at least two lines that must balance before it posts. Invoices, bills, receipts and payments generate their journals automatically, and you can see each one before and after it posts.

Can I fix a mistake in a posted entry?

You reverse it. Zycount posts an equal-and-opposite entry linked to the original, then you post the correct one. The ledger keeps the full story, which is exactly what an auditor wants to see.

Does it handle SST and LHDN e-Invoice?

Tax codes carry the rate and post output and input tax to separate accounts. Invoices can be submitted to LHDN MyInvois and show their validation status and QR code. Always confirm current rates and rules with the authorities before you file.

Can I move my existing books in?

You can set your own account codes and document numbers, enter opening balances, and import bank statements to reconcile against.

Can I try it right now?

Yes — create an account and your books open straight away, with a ready chart of accounts and this year's periods.

Who owns the data?

You do. Export reports and lists to CSV, print or download documents as PDF, and invite your accountant or auditor with a read-only role.
